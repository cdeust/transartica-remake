# Authored orchestral score

The original compositions and rendering code are licensed under the project's
MIT license. These compositions are newly authored for Transartica and do not
transcribe or reproduce the historical game's soundtrack.

Recorded instrument sources: **VSCO 2 Community Edition**, recorded by Sam
Gossner and Simon Dalzell; sample cutting by Elan Hickler/Soundemote.
The author releases this sample library under **CC0 1.0 Universal**:

- https://versilian-studios.com/vsco-community/
- https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE/440300901dfe9275fd84e0b7763af1f8443ae62e/LICENSE

This directory contains composed stereo masters and their cue manifest.
Individual instrument recordings and sample libraries are not included here.
Reproduction commands, sample hashes and measurements are documented in
`tasks/evidence/orchestral-score-20261006.md`.
