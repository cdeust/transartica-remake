# Wolves and mole men: source and acceptance

Source is the private unpacked English ECS TABLE/TIME/YODA/SCENE1/SCENE4/TEXTEK bytecode, cross-checked against pinned ALIS `opernames.c` and `opcodes.c`. No historical text or pixels enter public modules.

- TABLE `0x463..4b7` creates seven mole cells. TABLE `0xc4` clears ten signed timestamp buckets. TIME `0x2687` is reconstructed from raw stack operands, not the old lossy pretty-printer: `stamp == -1 OR (((day-1 > stamp/50) OR (day > stamp/50 AND hour >= stamp%50)) AND rnd(6)==0)`. Even a forced `-1` consumes the random draw. TIME `0x26d5` stamps `day*50+hour`, then sends127. Inspection cars share the same buckets.
- Wolf TABLE `0x56f..59e` chooses one of eleven source starts. TIME `0x85f..8ed` freezes when `6516 != 0`; otherwise every second callback advances phase, phase3 commits a cell and resets0, phase1 turns. Movement is shared with the world owner's source helper. Wolf history includes three source switch entries (TIME `0x145d..149b`, bound2 inclusive), unlike Viking trains' two.
- Successful Oslo CODE (SCENE4 `0x2fc..320`) places the frozen wolf at126,15 heading4. YODA `0x11c1→29dc..2af6` removes that presence after the final attack report, chooses a source relocation, sets heading6, resets counters and clears the freeze. Wolf bit8 is independent of Viking bit16, nomad4, herd2 and mole128.
- Player mole: TIME `0x266a..26f0→127`, YODA `0x183→155e`, SCENE4 selector2/resource24/palettes25+0. Wolf: TIME bit8, YODA `0xd5→115d`, SCENE1 selector2/resource14/palettes16+18. Original frames remain private; authored `wolf-ambush.png` and `mole-ambush.png` are integrated by scene name.
- TEXTEK `0x4407..454c` counts all type7 mammoths, functional type12 machine-gun wagons, and type23/24 soldiers. Divisor is `max(2,10-(soldiers+30*mammoths+60*machine_gun_wagons)/40)` with integer division. Reports77/78 advance to81 (human losses), then79 (wolf material losses) or80 (mole material losses), from `0x464c..46e1`.
- Human `0x1c9f..1e31`: each load loses integer quantity/divisor. Mammoth report intentionally reads the already reduced load before dividing again (`0x1cdc` then `0x1cf4`); the original underreport is retained. Wolf79 halves meat/salt/fish loads in merchandise wagons. Mole80 removes anthracite and lignite by the same divisor and, with more than four wagons, destroys the first type>3 other than tender21, regardless of its prior state. The original conditional display arithmetic and misleading temporary variable reuse are retained; printed fuel quantities follow raw AST registers, not the obsolete formatted listing.
- Spy observations store wolf60, nomad70 and herd40..49 (TIME `0x2191`). TEXTEK `0x287f..29f5` now formats these branches, including herd quantities rounded down to tens at20+, and source headings1..8.

Persistence adds optional fauna to the existing campaign schema1 inside outer save9. Legacy saves predate working wolf encounters: a known opened Oslo reconstructs the frozen blockade; pre-Oslo fauna initializes through the original ordered random setup. This migration cannot recover a historical roaming position absent from an older file. New saves retain position, phase, callback remainder, freeze and switch history. Ambush divisor, applied page and exact private report lines are saved; validator requires page/applied agreement before mutation. Human and material losses commit once when their source page begins, and rendering/restoring that page is passive.

`test_campaign_fauna.gd` executes native source callback counts, cooldown boundaries/random consumption, invalid snapshot rejection, source human-loss goldens, all three nonenemy spy reports, actual wolf/mole UI and transactional `.SAV` interruption/resume.
