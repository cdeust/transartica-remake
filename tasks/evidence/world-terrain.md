# Authored travel terrain

The original reference is `CARTE.FIC` and CARTE resource images65..151, independently inspected through the decoded resource atlas and the resource width/height/palette records in private `map-resources.json`. Original square map axes and rail traversal remain governed by map-orientation-audit.md and rail-network.md. The replacement art is MIT and generated by tools/build_travel_terrain.py without reading or sampling those historical resources. Only resource associations and geographic cell positions are retained.

Resource groups inspected:65 industrial station;71..76 six town pieces;77/80..85 landmarks;78/79 mine/open and closed;86..105 mountains/ice edges;106..115 water/shore;116..132 forest;133 industrial site;134 oasis;135..140 water/bridge variants;142..146 ice/mountains;147..148 landmarks;149 pool. Blank/unmapped images remain blank. The signed raw scenery bytes128..151 map through `code+256`, never through absolute-value traversal semantics; damaged rails retain RailGlyphs semantics. Concealed special sites consequently remain concealed until the campaign writes their actual tile.

Art decisions:96px cells; stepped snowcaps; one upper-left highlight direction; dense forest clumps; muted steel/pine shadows; warm town windows/chimneys; fine world-anchored wind-polished snow grain. These dimensions, palette values and seeded microtexture choices are authored presentation, not original gameplay constants. Town71..76 now have six distinct industrial compositions rather than repeating one building. Shorelines omit strokes at rectangular internal boundaries. No random landmark is added outside the source map.

TravelMapEntities inherits source city labels and enemy perception. Its override prevents historical raster tiles repainting the authored environment after rails. Terrain paints behind rails, trains and map markers. Root's RailArt loader/delegate copied verbatim into TravelWorld; rail-art files/assets remain root-owned and excluded from this commit.

Native Godot review captures actual start, mountain(8,8), lake(54,48), forest(80,11), town(63,42) views in tasks/validation/terrain-*.png. review_travel_terrain asserts all79 mapped scenery textures load, signed resource association and an unchanged full network snapshot. test_travel_world passes fixed-camera, train registration, switch input, geometry and interpolation checks. These are renderer and source-map checks, not a claim that the owner has approved the new artistic direction.

Save/reset integration corrections: WorldActions validates JSON numeric domains before converting day to int for modulo; MineTable normalizes validated rows to integer arrays, preserving deterministic snapshots after JSON. WorksDialog.reset clears accepted/pending close state and hides controls; a stale close cannot repair the new game's map. test_world_actions includes JSON roundtrip and rejected fractional snapshot; test_works_commit includes accepted-dialog reset then stale-close refusal.
